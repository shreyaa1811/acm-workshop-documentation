## How the workshop works

- **Four weeks:** Each week focuses on a different technology track.
- **Two sessions per week:** Learn the concepts, then apply them through practical activities.
- **Learn by doing:** Follow the lesson notes, examples, and exercises provided for each session.

## Getting started

Choose a week from the sidebar to explore its sessions. Start with Day 1 and work through the material in order.

Happy learning!
